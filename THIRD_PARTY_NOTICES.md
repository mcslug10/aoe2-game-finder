## aoe2-openings (classification rules)

The strategy-opening rule structure and several public thresholds adapted in `lib/aoe2-coach-evaluation.ts` are based on the MIT-licensed project:

- Project: [kaankiziltug/aoe2-openings](https://github.com/kaankiziltug/aoe2-openings)
- Copyright (c) 2026 Kaan Kızıltuğ
- License: MIT

The original project documents transparent replay-based opening rules and their limits. This repository adapts a subset of those rules to the per-player data returned by the AoE II Companion API. The scoring, reaction heuristics, wording, and UI in AOE2 Game Finder are separate additions.
